ContentForge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Scope

ContentForge is a private tool used by a single individual (the "owner") on the owner's own computer. It has no other users, no public website and no accounts for third parties. This policy describes what the tool does with the data of the accounts the owner connects to it: the owner's TikTok account (sections 2 to 5) and the owner's YouTube channels (section 7).

2. What we access

  • Basic profile information of the connected TikTok account (display name and identifier), through the user.info.basic scope.
  • Video upload to the account's inbox (drafts), through the video.upload scope.
  • Optionally, the list and public statistics of the owner's own videos (video.list), to show the owner how the videos perform.

3. How it is stored

The authorization tokens issued by TikTok are stored encrypted, in a database that runs on the owner's own computer. They are never written to logs, never sent to any server other than TikTok's, and never shared. Videos and statistics stay on the same computer.

4. Sharing and selling

No data is sold, rented, shared with, or disclosed to any third party. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking services.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the owner uses the tool. The owner can disconnect the account at any time inside the tool, which deletes the stored tokens, and can also revoke the app's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions). Stored statistics can be deleted by the owner at any time.

6. Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.

7. YouTube and Google user data

When the owner connects one of the owner's own YouTube channels, ContentForge receives OAuth tokens from Google and, with the scopes granted, uses the YouTube API Services to: upload the owner's finished videos to that channel (youtube.upload), read the processing status and visibility of those uploads (youtube.readonly) and read the channel's own performance metrics, such as views and retention (yt-analytics.readonly).

This data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to plan the owner's future videos. It is not sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising. ContentForge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) on the owner's own computer and are deleted, and revoked at Google, when the owner disconnects the channel; access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

ContentForge uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
