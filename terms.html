ContentForge — Terms of Service

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. What ContentForge is

ContentForge is a private tool, written and operated by a single individual (the "owner"), that prepares short videos and sends them to the owner's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public, has no other users and does not provide any service to third parties.

2. Who may use it

Only the owner. The app connects only to accounts that the owner controls, using the platforms' official authorization flows (for TikTok, Login Kit and the Content Posting API). Videos are sent to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts; the owner reviews each one inside the TikTok app and decides whether to post or discard it.

3. Content

The owner is responsible for the videos and for respecting TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Content Sharing Guidelines, as well as the rights of anyone whose material appears in them. The tool does not publish anything on its own initiative in this configuration.

ContentForge is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. The owner may change or stop using it at any time.
